About

An ambassador of Jesus Christ, raising disciples for the Kingdom

A trained Medical Doctor, called to be an Apostle of Jesus Christ, passionately committed to leading people into a deeper relationship with God and equipping believers to fulfil their divine purpose.

Dr. Imaobong Odutola’s passion for spreading the Gospel was ignited during her high school years through a powerful divine encounter with God that marked the beginning of her apostolic assignment. Since then, she has remained committed to impacting lives, transforming destinies, and advancing the Kingdom of God through the preaching of the Gospel and the demonstration of the power of the Holy Spirit.

She faithfully served as a leader in Christ Embassy from her university days. She now leads Light and Grace Ministries, with a mandate to give light, the knowledge of salvation and His eternal plan for mankind in the demonstration of His Spirit and Power.

Purpose is not discovered through human ambition, but through intimacy with God and obedience to His calling. Dr. Imaobong Odutola
IO
The work

Revival, prayer and purpose

Through Light and Grace Ministries, she and her team host revival gatherings in different cities of the world, preaching the gospel of Jesus, awakening believers to the person and ministry of the Holy Spirit, and witnessing many saved, healed, delivered and empowered.

She is the convener of Prophetic Prayer and Praise, a prophetic prayer movement birthed to awaken intimacy with the Lord through fervent prayer, heartfelt worship, and deep encounters with the Holy Spirit. Through her live prayer platforms, countless lives across nations have experienced spiritual awakening, healing, deliverance, restoration, and a renewed hunger for God.

She is also the visionary behind The Change Woman Global, a divine mandate and global movement commissioned to ignite prayer, awaken the power of the Holy Spirit, and stir purpose in women. Through conferences, teachings, mentorship and prophetic gatherings, women are empowered to rise as vessels of influence, revival, leadership and Kingdom impact in their generation.

An accomplished author, she has written several life-transforming books, including Make the Change, The Praying Grace and The Laws of Favour, as well as the monthly Make the Change Devotional.

Through her profound, revelatory and Spirit-breathed teachings, Dr. Imaobong Odutola continues to raise disciples, awaken revival, and lead many into transformational encounters with Jesus Christ and the power of the Holy Spirit. Based in Australia, she lives with her husband and three children.
