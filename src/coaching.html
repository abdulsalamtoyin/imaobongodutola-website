Coaching

Discover, embrace and fulfil God’s purpose for your life

Dr. Imaobong Odutola is a certified Purpose Coach, visionary leader and Apostle of Jesus Christ, with a divine mandate to help individuals find and walk in their assignment.

With deep spiritual insight and transformational coaching, she has become a voice of clarity and direction to many seeking meaning, identity and destiny in Christ.

Her passion for purpose discovery is rooted in the belief that every individual was created intentionally by God and carries a unique assignment designed to impact generations. Through her teachings, mentorship, coaching programs, prayer platforms and prophetic ministrations, she empowers people to break free from limitation, fear, confusion and stagnation.

IO
The approach

Practical wisdom, biblical principle, prophetic insight

As a Purpose Coach, she has guided countless individuals into discovering their God-given identity, unlocking hidden potential, and aligning their lives with Heaven’s divine agenda. Her coaching combines practical wisdom, biblical principles, prophetic insight and spiritual mentorship, equipping people to live intentionally and fulfil their calling with excellence and impact.

Dr. Imaobong believes that purpose is not merely about career or ambition, but about becoming everything God has ordained and using one’s life as a vessel to reveal Christ to the world. Her message consistently inspires people to pursue intimacy with the Holy Spirit, embrace personal transformation, and walk confidently in the fullness of their assignment.

Through global conferences, mentorship communities, discipleship initiatives and transformational teachings, she continues to raise purpose-driven believers who are spiritually awakened, empowered by the Holy Spirit, and committed to advancing God’s Kingdom in every sphere of influence.
